    Ketentuan pertandinganCompetition rules

    Turnamen memakai Rules of Golf R&A, Local Rules komite dan Local Rules Damai Indah Golf BSD Course. Keputusan komite bersifat mutlak.Played under the R&A Rules of Golf, the committee's local rules and Damai Indah Golf BSD Course's local rules. The committee's decision is final.

    Penentuan pemenang bila seriBreaking ties

    Gross: count back 9, 6, 3 dan 1 hole terakhir di back nine (hole 10–18), lalu cara yang sama di front nine (hole 1–9).count back the last 9, 6, 3 and 1 holes on the back nine (holes 10–18), then the same on the front nine (holes 1–9).

    Nett: count back hole 10–18: gross 9 hole − ½ hcp, 6 hole − ⅓ hcp, 3 hole − ⅙ hcp, hole 18 − 1/9 hcp. Bila masih sama, komite mengundi.count back holes 10–18: 9-hole gross − ½ hcp, 6 holes − ⅓, 3 holes − ⅙, hole 18 − 1/9. If still tied, the committee draws lots.

    Pemain yang tidak menyelesaikan turnamen dianggap "No Record".Players who don't finish are recorded as "No Record".

    Local rules
    1. Ball in circle: bola di dalam lingkaran boleh di-holing out atau diangkat dengan tambahan 1 pukulan.a ball inside the circle may be holed out or picked up for 1 extra stroke.
    2. Dropping zone (MLR E-1): di hole 3, 8 dan 11, dengan 1 pukulan penalti untuk pembebasan dari penalty area.on holes 3, 8 and 11, with a 1-stroke penalty for relief from a penalty area.
    3. Lost ball / OB (MLR E-5): drop di area pembebasan dengan tambahan 2 pukulan.drop in the relief area with a 2-stroke penalty.
    4. Preferred lies (MLR E-3): di area berumput setinggi fairway atau lebih pendek, bola boleh diangkat, dibersihkan dan ditempatkan dalam 1 lebar scorecard, tidak mendekati hole, tanpa penalti.on grass cut to fairway height or shorter, the ball may be lifted, cleaned and placed within one scorecard length, no nearer the hole, without penalty.
    5. Boundary edges (MLR A-1): bila patok putih bersebelahan dengan cart path, batas OB adalah tepi luar cart path (titik putih).where white stakes run beside a cart path, the outer edge of the cart path (white dots) is out of bounds.
    Tempo permainanPace of play
    • Call on di setiap par 3.Call on at every par 3.
    • Pukul dalam 40 detik saat giliran Anda.Play within 40 seconds when it's your turn.
    • Tertinggal dari group depan? Mainkan ready golf. Marshal berwenang menjaga tempo.Falling behind the group ahead? Play ready golf. Marshals are authorised to keep the pace.
    Cuaca buruk & sireneBad weather & sirens

    1× sirene: permainan dihentikan sementara · 2×: dilanjutkan · 3×: dihentikan total. Semua peserta kembali ke clubhouse. Status terbaru tampil di bar di atas halaman ini.1 siren: play suspended · 2: play resumes · 3: play terminated. Everyone returns to the clubhouse.
